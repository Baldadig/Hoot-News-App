Hoot News is an independent, calm news reader for Dutch-speaking readers. No account is needed: every feature works right after launch.

What the app does
- Edition: three times a day (06:00, 12:00, 17:00 Amsterdam time) a fixed edition of ten stories, composed by our own server from the news sources the reader follows.
- Compare: when several outlets cover the same story, "+N bronnen" shows how each of them reports it.
- Stream: all items from the reader's chosen sources, newest first. Sources can be switched on and off.
- Digest: every Monday the three most important professional articles of the week per field (paid social, UI/UX, digital design), chosen by how many trade publications covered them and by human curation.
- Saved and Search work on the device.

Where the content comes from
- Headlines, short introductions and images come from the publishers' own public RSS feeds and from the share image they publish for each article. Hoot never shows the full article: tapping a story always opens the article on the publisher's website, in SFSafariViewController (or Safari, if the reader prefers). Paywalled publishers stay paywalled.
- The app talks only to our own server (https://hootnews.nl), which also powers the website.

Privacy
- No account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. Preferences, saved items and read state are stored on the device only.

Navigating the app
- Tabs: Edition, Stream, Digest, Saved, Search. Settings: the gear button at the top right.
- On iPad and on wide windows the layout switches to two columns and the tab bar can become a sidebar.
